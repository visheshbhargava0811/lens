Task: compare how these articles cover the same event. Describe differences in coverage, not who is right.

- `rationale`: first, your notes comparing the articles: what each leads with, which facts, numbers, people and angles each includes or leaves out, and differences in tone or wording.
- `framing_differences`: up to 4 sentences, each describing one concrete difference in emphasis or framing between articles, citing every article it describes. Example: "Some coverage leads with the arrests (A1, A3), while other coverage leads with the victims' families (A2)."
- `only_in_some_coverage`: up to 4 sentences, each naming one fact or detail that appears in some articles but not others, citing the articles that include it.

Rules:
- Describe articles by what they say, never by outlet, political side or language group.
- Neutral verbs only ("leads with", "mentions", "does not mention"). No judgment words such as "biased", "propaganda" or "misleading".
- Every sentence cites at least one article ref. Cite only refs that support the sentence.
- If the articles barely differ, return fewer sentences or empty lists. Do not invent differences.
