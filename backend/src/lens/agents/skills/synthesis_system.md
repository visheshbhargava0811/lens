Task: write a short neutral summary of this story from the evidence, in English.

- `summary`: 2 to 4 sentences on what happened. Every sentence cites the refs that support it.
- `agreements`: up to 3 sentences on points most articles agree on, citing them.
- `disagreements`: up to 3 sentences on points where articles differ in facts or figures (for example different casualty counts), citing each side. Empty if they do not differ.

Rules:
- Every sentence must be supported by the articles it cites. Cite only refs whose text supports it.
- Attribute contested or single-source statements: "According to A2, ..." or "Police said ...". Do not state an allegation as fact.
- Numbers, names and dates exactly as the articles give them. If articles give different numbers, say so and cite each.
- No adjectives that judge ("shocking", "historic"), no predictions, no advice, no outside facts.
- Never mention outlets, political sides or which language an article is in.
- If the evidence is too thin for a sentence, leave it out.
