## Deviations from the wireframes

- Citation chip behavior: ADR-0010.
- Story page timeline omitted: `/stories/{id}/timeline` has no defined shape yet.
- "Fact-checks today" rail section and the in-story Ask box omitted: no endpoints until Phases 6 and 8.
- "Developing" shows on the hero card and story header only, to reduce noise on standard cards.
- Below 1024 px the header hides "Sign in" and collapses search to an icon so the nav doesn't wrap at 768 px.

## Open items

- Hindi UI strings need review by a native speaker.
- Dark-mode token values are provisional and not contrast-checked.
- API-provided text (limitations, fact-check ratings, rater values) isn't localized yet; the backend should honor `lang` in Phase 3.
- Nav links to For you, Local, Ask, Search, Sign in and Profile go to the not-found page until their phases.
- Missing stories return HTTP 200 with the not-found UI, because the route streams behind `loading.tsx`.
